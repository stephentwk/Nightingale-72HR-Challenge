Record the browser at roughly 1440×900 with the server terminal hidden. Use synthetic data only.

Start in Clinician view on Maya Chen. Say: “Nightingale is a shared memory for the visit. The first screen is intentionally an attention budget, not a new wall of notes.” Point to the one top signal, the three open loops, and the 10-second view label.

## 0:15–0:35 — Glance → evidence

Click **Open source** on Chest pressure. Let the timeline scroll to the April 15, 2025 AI doctor consult and show the exact phrase highlight. Point out the AI-generated badge, source session/transcript ID, confidence label, and “no diagnosis is asserted” disclaimer.

## 0:35–0:52 — Human confirmation and learning

Click **Keep** on the AI patient-session signal. Show the toast, learning count, and trust ledger. Say: “This feedback changes ranking for similar content; it cannot learn away high-risk floors, and it never auto-publishes patient copy.”

## 0:52–1:10 — Collaboration and revision

Show the diff and revert to v1. Open **Audit trail** and point out that it records who/what/which version metadata, not note content.

## 1:10–1:25 — Role boundary

Switch to **Patient**. Say: “This is not a CSS hide.” Show the two approved entries, prep booklet, and the absence of raw AI summaries and internal comments. Switch to Staff if desired to show the coordination surface.

## 1:25–1:40 — Capture and close

Click **Capture consult**. Read the consent → redact → provenance steps and submit the synthetic redacted preview. Point out that the new AI-scribed entry is source-linked and the audit records `raw_audio_stored=false`.

Close with: “The prototype optimizes for fewer clicks, but every shortcut lands on a source of truth.”
